Four core digital pillars drive end-to-end operational excellence across BSL plants and mines

Synthesis of strategic levers, technical architecture, and flagship shop-floor implementations
Transformation Pillar Description & Core Strategic Levers Select BSL Examples & Quantified Impact
1. Primary Steelmaking & Blast Furnace AI~INR 550 Cr | 48 Initiatives
  • Closed-loop thermal state prediction and burden distribution modeling in Blast Furnaces
  • Dynamic charge-mix and ferro-alloy minimization in Basic Oxygen Furnaces (SMS-II)
  • Automated sinter plant basicity and moisture control connected to raw material chemistry
  • BF Coke Rate AI: -14 kg/tHM reduction (+INR 240 Cr)
  • BOF First-Turndown Hit Rate: +18% improvement (+INR 165 Cr)
2. Rolling Mills & Vision Quality Control~INR 380 Cr | 42 Initiatives
  • Pacing optimization and reheating furnace fuel control in Hot Strip Mill (HSM)
  • High-speed Computer Vision defect detection to prevent downstream cold-rolling cobbles
  • Vibration and thermal anomaly prediction across mill stands, bearings, and coilers
  • HSM Bottleneck Pacing: +6.5% throughput (+INR 190 Cr)
  • CV Surface Inspection: -31% quality downgrades (+INR 90 Cr)
3. Smart Energy, Utilities & Mine-to-Mill~INR 260 Cr | 36 Initiatives
  • Real-time optimization of BF, Coke Oven, and LD gas network to minimize flaring
  • Integrated mine-to-pellet-to-blast-furnace logistics scheduling and rake turnaround
  • Smart water and oxygen plant load forecasting synchronized with converter blowing
  • Mine-to-Sinter Blend: +INR 145 Cr iron recovery
  • Gas Grid Dispatch: -28% gas flaring loss (+INR 70 Cr)
4. OT/IT Data Platform & Digital Academy~INR 120 Cr | 28 Enablers
  • Unified industrial data lakehouse ingesting Level-1/Level-2 PLC & SCADA tags at 1-sec resolution
  • GenAI Maintenance Copilot indexing 15+ years of BSL OEM manuals and breakdown logs
  • Dedicated Digital Center of Excellence (CCoE) upskilling 450+ plant engineers and translators
  • Unified OT/IT Lake: 85% sensor tag coverage by Q3 FY27
  • SAIL Digital Academy: 60+ agile pod specialists trained
